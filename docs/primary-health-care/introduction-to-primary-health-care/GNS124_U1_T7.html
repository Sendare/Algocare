Development Committees in Primary Health Care Services in Nigeria

Introduction to Development Committees in Nigerian Primary Health Care

Primary Health Care (PHC) in Nigeria relies heavily on community participation to succeed. Under the Ward Minimum Health Care Package, governance structures are established at different tiers to bring decision-making down to the grassroot level. These structures are known as Development Committees. By involving local leaders, traditional rulers, and community members, these groups ensure that health interventions actually match the real needs of the people. They bridge the gap between healthcare providers and the community, fostering trust and ownership.

Village Development Committee and Ward Development Committee

Operating at the settlement and political ward levels, these grassroots bodies are the foundation of community health governance.

  • Village Development Committee (VDC): Operates at the village level, identifying local health problems, mobilizing residents for community health action, and supporting outreach services.
  • Ward Development Committee (WDC): Coordinates activities across several villages within a political ward, serves as a link to the primary health center, and manages minor local resources for health facility maintenance.

Health Facility Development Committee

The Health Facility Development Committee (HFDC) works directly within a specific primary health center or clinic. Its membership includes health workers, facility-in-charges, and community representatives. Their core duty is to ensure the facility remains functional, clean, and welcoming to patients. They monitor drug availability, assist in managing user fees where applicable, and report infrastructure decay to higher authorities. When an HFDC functions well, absenteeism among health staff drops because the community is actively watching over the facility.

District and Local Government Area Committees

Moving up the administrative ladder, broader coordination becomes necessary to link multiple wards and districts.

  • District Development Committee: Coordinates health activities across a district, harmonizing plans from various wards and passing them to the local government level.
  • Local Government Area (LGA) PHC Development Committee: Chaired by the LGA Chairman or supervisory councillor for health, this body manages resources, coordinates staffing, and ensures budget allocations for primary health care within the entire local government area.

State and National Implementation Bodies

At the macro level, state and national committees provide policy direction, technical support, and funding oversight. The State PHC Management Board works alongside implementation committees to supervise LGA activities, procure bulk medical supplies, and ensure state health policies align with national guidelines. Nationally, coordinating bodies set standard operating procedures, evaluate nationwide health indicators, and collaborate with international donor agencies. These higher tiers ensure that grassroots efforts are sustained by solid national infrastructure.

Challenges and Strategies for Effective Committee Operations

Despite their crucial role, development committees in Nigeria face persistent hurdles. Common barriers include poor funding, political interference, lack of transport for outreach, and high rates of illiteracy among community members which can limit active participation. Many committee members serve purely on a voluntary basis without incentives, leading to burnout.

To overcome these challenges, community mobilization must be continuous, leadership training should be provided regularly, and transparent financial record-keeping must be enforced to build lasting trust.
