Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account on Algocare, we collect your Name, Email Address, and Username. We also store the data you provide in your profile (Age, Gender, Institution, Bio) and your exam performance data (scores, questions answered, time spent).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve the Algocare platform. This includes generating your dashboard statistics, displaying your rank on the leaderboard, and personalizing your study experience. We do not sell your data to third parties.

3. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures, including Supabase authentication and secure Cloudflare Workers, to protect your data. Your passwords are encrypted and never visible to us.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active. You may request account deletion at any time by contacting us.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at algotred.a1v1@gmail.com.
